## How does academic credit transfer work under UGC guidelines

Under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and UGC guidelines, students who have successfully earned credits in previous semesters at an accredited university are entitled to credit recognition.

Through the **Academic Bank of Credits (ABC ID)** and university lateral entry provisions:
- Subjects you previously passed at a recognized university with passing grades are mapped against the curriculum of the receiving university.
- Equivalent credits are transferred, meaning you do not have to retake subjects you already cleared.
- You only need to appear for the remaining semester examinations required to satisfy the university's graduation requirements.
- The degree certificate is awarded directly by the UGC-recognized university, with no mention of "dropout" or "irregular" on the final parchment.

## Are degrees obtained through Get My Degree valid for government jobs and PSC

**For the Indian partner universities, yes — with the usual conditions.** Each is recognized by the University Grants Commission, but not all in the same way: Bharathidasan University is a state university recognized under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, PRIST is a deemed-to-be university, and Rabindranath Tagore University is a state private university. Marjon University is in the United Kingdom, so the UGC does not recognize it; a UK degree usually needs [AIU equivalence](https://aiu.ac.in/) before it counts for Indian government posts.

The [UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020](https://www.ugc.gov.in/) treat distance and online degrees from recognized institutions as equivalent to campus degrees, provided the university held a valid [DEB](https://deb.ugc.ac.in/) entitlement for that programme in your admission year. Always check that entitlement before you enrol.

With those conditions met, a degree from an Indian partner university is accepted for:
- **UPSC Civil Services** (IAS, IPS, IFS)
- **State PSC Examinations** (including Kerala Public Service Commission)
- **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** and **Railway Recruitment Board (RRB)**
- **Banking Exams** (SBI PO, IBPS Clerk/PO, RBI)
- **Higher Education Admissions** (regular campus Master’s, M.Phil, or Ph.D. programs)
- **WES (World Education Services)** evaluation for international credential verification.

The exception is any post whose notification or Special Rules require a **regular course of study**. For those, a distance degree does not qualify; the Kerala High Court confirmed this in January 2026 for Librarian Grade IV (*Kerala PSC v. Vaisakh C.L.*, 2026:KER:6103). Check the rules for your post before you enrol.
