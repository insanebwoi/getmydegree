A postgraduate degree while employed is ordinary, not heroic. Large numbers of people do it every year. The ones who finish tend to have made the same few decisions early.

## Check eligibility before anything else

A master's programme requires a completed bachelor's degree from a recognized university, usually with a minimum aggregate that varies by university and programme. Some PG programmes require the UG degree to be in a related discipline; many, including most MBA and MA programmes, accept any discipline.

If your UG degree has a pending backlog, resolve that first. An incomplete bachelor's degree is the most common reason a PG application stops, and it is the one thing no counsellor can work around.

## The formats, and who each suits

Self-paced online programmes release recorded lectures and material you work through on your own schedule, with assessment at fixed points. They suit irregular or shift work, and they demand the most self-discipline.

Weekend or evening live sessions give you a fixed timetable and a cohort. They suit people whose working week is predictable and who study better with a scheduled obligation than with a deadline three weeks out.

Open and distance learning sits between the two: printed or digital material, periodic contact sessions, and examination at the end of each semester.

## What the week really looks like

Budget eight to twelve hours a week for a PG programme, unevenly spread. Assignment weeks and the fortnight before examinations take considerably more. The rest of the semester takes less than you expect.

The pattern that works for most people: one fixed weekday evening, one longer weekend block, and a plan for the deadline weeks agreed with your family before the semester begins rather than during it.

## Choosing the specialization

At PG level the specialization is the part employers read, so it should point where you are going. An MBA in operations reads differently from one in human resources, and a promotions committee notices. If you do not yet know, a general management or commerce PG keeps more doors open than a narrow specialization aimed at a role you are not sure you want.

## Before you commit

Confirm three things in writing: the university's entitlement to run that programme in that mode for the current session, the total fee including examination charges, and the examination pattern. Those three decide whether the degree counts, what it costs and whether it fits your year.
