A particular call comes in regularly. Twelve or fifteen years in the role, running a team, genuinely good at the work, and passed over for a management position because the file does not show a degree. It is a frustrating way to be stopped, and it is one of the more fixable problems we deal with.

## Find out exactly what is required

Before choosing anything, get the criterion in writing from HR or your personnel section. There is a real difference between "a bachelor's degree in any discipline", "a graduate degree in a relevant field" and "a postgraduate management qualification". Each points at a different programme and a different amount of time.

Ask two further questions while you are there. Does the mode matter, and is a degree in progress acceptable for consideration or must it be complete. Some organisations will consider a candidate who is enrolled; many will not. That answer changes your timeline more than anything else.

## Count what you already have

A large share of people in this position started a degree and stopped. Those completed semesters usually retain credit value at a recognized university, and a receiving university can admit you with advanced standing against equivalent papers.

In practice that can mean finishing one year rather than three. Before assuming you are starting from zero, gather every marksheet you hold and have them assessed. It is the single change that most often shortens this.

## Choose for speed and certainty, not prestige

The purpose here is narrow: satisfy a written criterion so the file clears the filter. The degree that does that soonest, from a properly recognized university, is the right one. An ambitious programme that takes two years longer serves you worse, and nobody on a promotions panel is comparing university rankings against your fifteen years of performance.

## Keep the evidence

When the degree is awarded, put the certificate, consolidated marksheet and evidence of the university's recognition and entitlement for your session into your personnel file together. Verification happens at promotion, sometimes years later, and having the entitlement evidence already on file avoids a delay at exactly the wrong moment.
