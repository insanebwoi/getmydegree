Open and distance learning and online learning are frequently used as though they meant the same thing. They are separate modes, regulated separately, and they produce a noticeably different experience.

## Open and distance learning

The older model. The university provides study material, printed or digital, you work through it largely on your own, and there are periodic contact sessions at a study centre. Assessment is usually a written examination at the end of each semester, often at a physical centre, sometimes with assignments contributing.

It suits people with limited or unreliable internet, people who prefer reading material to watching lectures, and people who are comfortable with a single concentrated examination rather than continuous deadlines.

## Online learning

Delivered through a learning platform: recorded or live lectures, digital material, discussion forums, and assessment through a mix of continuous assignments, quizzes and remotely proctored examinations.

It suits people with reliable connectivity, people who want to study in short blocks across the week, and people whose schedule will not survive travelling to a centre for examinations.

## The differences that actually matter

- Where you sit examinations. Centre-based examinations mean travel on fixed dates. Remote proctoring means a laptop, a camera and a quiet room. For shift workers and people who travel, this is often the whole decision.

- How the workload is spread. Continuous assessment spreads it evenly and punishes a bad month. A single end-of-semester examination concentrates it and punishes a bad fortnight.

- How much structure you get. Online programmes with live sessions give you a timetable. Distance programmes give you material and a date, and the pacing is yours.

- Connectivity. An online programme with unreliable internet is a genuinely bad experience, and no amount of enthusiasm fixes it.

## What is the same

Both require the university to hold entitlement for that programme in that mode. Both produce a degree treated as equivalent to a campus one when that entitlement is in place. Neither is a lesser qualification.

## Choosing

Answer three questions honestly. Can you reach an examination centre on a fixed date. Is your internet reliable enough for proctored assessment. Do you work better against frequent small deadlines or one large one.
