A recognized Indian degree earned from abroad is a common requirement for Indians working overseas, particularly in the Gulf, where a degree is often needed for a visa category, a promotion or a professional licence. The process has a few extra steps and none of them are difficult if handled in the right order.

## Eligibility and prior qualifications

The entry requirement is the same as for a domestic applicant: a completed qualifying examination or degree from a recognized institution. The additional question for overseas applicants is whether a qualification earned in another country is accepted as equivalent.

Where your prior schooling or degree is from outside India, the university may ask for an equivalence certificate from the appropriate Indian body. Ask about this before applying rather than after, because obtaining it takes time and it is the step that most often delays an overseas admission.

Qualifications from Indian curriculum schools abroad, which is the common case in the Gulf, are usually straightforward and need no equivalence process.

## Examinations from another country

This is the practical question. Remotely proctored examinations can generally be taken from anywhere with a stable connection, which is what makes studying from abroad workable at all.

Two things to confirm in writing: whether the university permits examinations to be taken from outside India, and what the examination times are in your local time zone. A paper scheduled for a mid-morning slot in India can fall inconveniently elsewhere, and it is better to know that before enrolling than during the first semester.

Where a physical centre is required, ask whether the university has an overseas centre arrangement. Many do not, and that decides the choice of programme.

## Fees and payment

Ask whether there is a separate fee structure for overseas applicants, which is common, and how payment is accepted from abroad. Get the total in writing, including examination fees and the cost of the certificate.

## Attestation, which people forget

If the degree will be used abroad for a visa, a licence or an employer, it will usually need attestation, which is a sequence: the issuing authorities in India, then the Ministry of External Affairs, then the embassy or consulate of the country where it will be used.

Start this as soon as the degree is issued. It takes weeks, and it is easier to complete while you still have the documents to hand than in the middle of a visa application.
