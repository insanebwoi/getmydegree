You can study for an Indian degree while living abroad, but in practice online mode is the route built for it, and the arrangements vary by university. UGC regulations let a learner residing inside or outside India enrol in a recognized online programme. Open and distance learning (ODL) is written around learners residing in India, with examinations held inside the university's territorial jurisdiction, and the UGC has told institutions not to enrol foreign nationals in ODL. So before you compare subjects, get the university to confirm in writing that it will admit someone in your position, in that mode, for that session.

Many of the people who ask us about this are working in the Gulf and need a degree for a promotion, a job change or a move home. The process has a few extra steps. None of them is difficult if you handle them in the right order, but some of what you will hear from agents is simply wrong, so here is how it actually works.

## Can NRIs and foreign nationals do an Indian distance degree?

It depends on your status and the mode, and the rules are tighter for ODL than most people expect. The table below reflects our reading of the UGC (ODL and Online Programmes) Regulations 2020 and a 2024 UGC notice. Your university's written answer still comes first.

| Your situation | Online mode | Open and distance learning |
|---|---|---|
| Indian citizen living abroad | Regulations allow learners outside India to enrol. Confirm that the university accepts overseas learners | The rule refers to learners residing in India, and examinations stay within its jurisdiction. Expect to be told you must be in India. Ask before applying |
| Foreign national | Possible only if the university is recognized to enrol international learners. Ask to see this | UGC has told institutions not to enrol foreign nationals |
| OCI cardholder | Ask the university how it classifies you | Ask the university. The reports we read do not settle this |
| Indian citizen living in Kerala now, moving abroad later | Usually the easier mode to carry with you | Possible while resident, but plan for coming back for examinations |

Our guide to [how online and distance modes differ](/blog/online-vs-distance-degree-modes) covers the rest of the comparison. Engineering, medicine, nursing, pharmacy, law and architecture are not permitted in these modes at all, whichever country you live in.

## Can I write Indian university exams from the Gulf or another country?

Only if the programme is online and the university confirms that it runs proctored examinations for learners abroad. The regulations ask universities recognized for international learners to endeavour to provide these, which is not the same as every university offering them for every paper. For ODL, plan on being in India for your examinations.

Ask for these answers in writing:

- Can every paper, including any practical or viva, be taken from outside India?

- What identification is checked? The regulations refer to passports for international learners and Aadhaar or other government identifiers for Indian learners.

- What equipment and room are required, and what happens if the connection drops mid-paper?

- If you have to travel to India for a paper, how much notice do you get?

Then work out the time. India is one and a half hours ahead of the UAE and Oman, and two and a half hours ahead of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. A 10am paper in India starts at 8.30am in Dubai and 7.30am in Riyadh, which may collide with a morning shift. In North America, the same paper may fall in the middle of your night. Check the actual timetable rather than assuming that it can be moved.

Here is an illustrative composite, not a real student. **Faisal, 33, a storekeeper in Jeddah from Tirur**, wants a B.Com for a supervisor post. His cousin recommended a distance programme because a relative had done one years ago. Once he asks where the papers are held, he sees that he would need leave and flights every semester, and he asks about online mode instead. He also asks his employer whether an online degree meets its rule, because that is a separate question.

## What documents do I need to apply from abroad?

You need the same qualifying documents as any other applicant, plus proof of identity and sometimes an equivalence step. The entry requirement stays the same: 12th pass from a recognized board for UG, and a bachelor's degree for PG. What differs is the paperwork around it.

- **Basic set.** SSLC and plus-two certificates and marksheets, any degree certificate and marksheets, passport copy and photograph. Ask whether scans are enough or originals must be shown.

- **Indian curriculum school in the Gulf.** A CBSE or other Indian board certificate is an Indian qualification, so a foreign equivalence step should not normally arise.

- **Foreign school board.** A British, American or IB certificate, or a certificate from a local ministry, may need equivalence. UGC's 2025 regulations set conditions for accepting foreign school qualifications for UG admission, so ask the university what it requires before you apply.

- **Foreign bachelor's degree, for a PG.** Equivalence moved from the AIU to the UGC under regulations notified in April 2025. It is not automatic, and qualifications obtained through franchising arrangements are excluded. Start early, because this is the step that most often delays an overseas admission.

- **An unfinished degree in India.** Old marksheets can matter, as our [credit transfer guide](/credit-transfer) explains, subject to the receiving university's rules.

Check that your name is spelled the same across your passport, certificates and application. A small difference in initials can become a real problem at attestation or at a Gulf employer's check, so correct it before you enrol.

A family member in Kerala can often help with collecting documents, but the application details and declarations are yours. Ask the university what must be signed personally.

## Will an Indian online degree be accepted in the country where I live?

That varies by country, employer and post, and this is the question to settle before you pay anything. Being recognized in India does not settle how the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar or anywhere else will treat a distance or online degree, particularly for government posts and licensed work. Our guide to [whether an online degree is valid for Gulf jobs](/blog/online-degree-valid-gulf-jobs) goes through this country by country.

Back in India, validity rests on the usual three things: the university's recognition, the programme's DEB entitlement for your admission year, and the wording of the job notification.

Attestation is a separate step, and a certificate being attested does not mean the degree is recognized. If your employer or visa needs it, the usual sequence runs through state-level verification, then the Ministry of External Affairs, then an apostille or embassy attestation depending on the country. Plan for this while the documents are within reach. Our [step-by-step guide to degree attestation for the Gulf](/blog/degree-attestation-gulf) has the details, including why a certificate from a university outside Kerala follows a different route.

## What varies from one university to another?

Quite a lot, which is why we will not give you one answer for every university. Ask each one directly about these:

- Whether overseas learners are accepted at all, and in which mode.

- Whether there is a separate fee structure for overseas applicants, and how payment from abroad is accepted. Get the full total in writing, including examination and certificate fees.

- Whether your country has any examination arrangements, and the rule on deferral if you cannot sit a paper.

- What the last date is for your session, since documents from abroad take longer.
