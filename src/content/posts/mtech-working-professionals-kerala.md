Yes, you can do an M.Tech in Kerala while keeping your job, through an M.Tech for working professionals at an AICTE-approved college. These are regular, in-person programmes with classes timed for employed engineers, usually in the evenings or at weekends. In December 2023 it was reported that AICTE had sanctioned postgraduate courses for working professionals at 54 engineering colleges in India, six of them in Kerala, and some Kerala colleges list such programmes on their admission pages.

What you cannot do is earn a valid M.Tech by distance or online mode. Engineering is excluded from distance and online study under the UGC's regulations, at postgraduate level as much as undergraduate. This guide explains what the working-professional M.Tech looks like, who qualifies, and how to check a programme before you apply.

## What is an M.Tech for working professionals?

It is a regular M.Tech, approved by AICTE and awarded by the affiliating university, in which classes are held at times an employed engineer can attend. You study in person at the college, follow the same kind of syllabus and examinations, and receive an M.Tech.

The difference from a full-time M.Tech is the timetable and the admission condition. For example, College of Engineering Thalassery describes its working-professional M.Tech as having classes "normally" in the evening hours or at other flexible convenient timings, including weekends and holidays, and requires applicants to be in full-time employment.

## Which Kerala colleges offer an M.Tech for working professionals?

There is no single official list we can point you to, and programmes open and close from year to year. What we can share are examples from verified sources, to show what exists.

- **College of Engineering Thalassery** lists an M.Tech in Embedded Systems and VLSI for working professionals, with an annual sanctioned intake of 30 seats, following KTU's M.Tech 2022 regulations.
- **Six colleges reported in December 2023.** Onmanorama reported that the six Kerala colleges with AICTE sanction for working-professional PG courses were Rajadhani Institute of Engineering and Technology, Attingal; Toc H Institute of Science and Technology, Kochi; Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology, Muvattupuzha; St Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology, Pala; Federal Institute of Science and Technology, Angamaly; and MES College of Engineering and Technology, Kuttippuram. Branches reported included structural and construction engineering, computer science, data science, AI and data science, renewable energy, instrumentation and control, and VLSI and embedded systems, with 15 seats per course.

That 2023 report describes one year's sanction. Before you plan around any of these, check the college's current admission page and AICTE's approval for 2026-27, because a programme approved once is not guaranteed to run every year.

## Who is eligible for an M.Tech for working professionals?

You generally need the academic eligibility for a regular M.Tech, plus proof that you are employed. The exact rules are set each year by the college, the university and, in Kerala, the Directorate of Technical Education's prospectus.

Taking College of Engineering Thalassery's page as an example, it states that:

- academic eligibility is the same as for the regular M.Tech, as per the prospectus issued annually by the DTE;
- applicants must have been in continuous full-time employment with a registered organisation for at least one year;
- an Employment Certificate and a conduct certificate are required;
- there is no age limit to apply.

Other colleges may add conditions, for example on the distance between your workplace and the college, which is a feature of the B.Tech working-professional norms. Check each college's notice.

## Do I need GATE for an M.Tech for working professionals?

Not necessarily, but check the prospectus. GATE is the main route into M.Tech programmes at government-supported institutions, and a GATE score can help with admission and financial assistance where it applies. The working-professional seats may follow different admission rules, which are set out in the year's prospectus and the college's notice.

## How much does it cost?

Fees vary by college and are revised every year, so treat any figure as an example only. As listed on College of Engineering Thalassery's working-professional M.Tech page, tuition is ₹40,000 a year, and the total payable at the college office is listed as ₹56,545, which the college notes may be revised once it receives the fee structure.

Self-financing colleges may charge differently. Ask for the full fee schedule in writing, including university fees, examination fees, caution deposits and any special fees, before you pay anything.

## Is a distance or online M.Tech valid?

No. The UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020 list engineering among the programmes that may not be offered in distance or online mode, and later amendments have not changed that. The Supreme Court's 2017 ruling on distance engineering degrees also held that technical education by distance could not continue without AICTE approval.

So an "M.Tech by distance" or "online M.Tech" offered through a study centre is not a valid engineering postgraduate degree in India, however it is advertised. Our guide to [distance B.Tech in Kerala](/blog/distance-btech-kerala) explains the same rules for undergraduate study, and [red flags before enrolling in an online degree](/blog/red-flags-before-enrolling-in-an-online-degree) lists the warning signs.

## Which M.Tech branch should a working engineer choose?

Choose the branch that fits the work you do now, or a clear next step from it. Working-professional programmes are most useful when what you learn in the evening connects with what you do at work during the day.

A few patterns to think about:

- **Civil and site engineers:** structural engineering, construction engineering and management.
- **Electrical engineers:** renewable energy, power and control-related branches, where offered.
- **Electronics engineers:** VLSI and embedded systems, an area linked to India's semiconductor push; see our article on VLSI and semiconductor careers after a B.Tech.
- **Software and IT engineers:** computer science, data science, or AI and data science.

Availability depends entirely on what is approved near you for the year.

## How do I fit an M.Tech around a full-time job?

Plan for a real time commitment. Evening and weekend classes, laboratory sessions, assignments and a project or thesis all have to fit around your working hours and your family.

Before you apply, talk to your employer: some will support study through flexible shifts, and some will expect you to manage on your own time.
