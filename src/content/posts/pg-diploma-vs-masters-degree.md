A postgraduate diploma takes less time and costs less than a master's degree, which makes it attractive to anyone in a hurry. Whether it is the right choice depends almost entirely on what you need it for.

## The structural difference

A master's degree is normally two years, carries the full postgraduate credit weight, and is a degree. A PG diploma is typically one year, carries fewer credits, and is a diploma.

That last distinction sounds pedantic and is the whole issue. Wherever a requirement is written as "a postgraduate degree", a diploma does not satisfy it, however good the diploma is.

## When a diploma is the right answer

When you want a specific competence rather than a credential: a defined skill set in a year, to do your current job better or to move sideways into a related function.

When you already hold a master's degree and want to add a specialisation without repeating a full programme.

When the requirement you are satisfying is your own, rather than an HR criterion written in a rule.

## When only a master's degree will do

When a promotion criterion, recruitment rule or notification specifies a postgraduate degree. Read the wording literally; this is the most common expensive mistake in this comparison.

When you may pursue a doctorate later, where a master's degree is normally the entry requirement.

When you intend to teach, where the degree requirements are specified and a diploma will not substitute.

When you may study abroad, where foreign institutions assess on credits and a diploma frequently falls short of what a master's-level entry requires.

## The question that settles it

Get the requirement in writing from whoever imposes it, and read the exact word used. "Postgraduate degree" and "postgraduate qualification" are not the same phrase, and the difference decides which programme you should enrol in.

If nobody is imposing a requirement and this is for your own development, the diploma's shorter duration is a genuine advantage and there is no reason to spend the extra year.

## One more option

Some universities allow a PG diploma to be carried forward into the related master's degree, with the diploma's credits counting toward it. Where that exists it is the best of both: a useful qualification in a year, with the option to complete the degree later without starting again. Ask whether the pathway exists before enrolling, because it has to be planned at the start.
